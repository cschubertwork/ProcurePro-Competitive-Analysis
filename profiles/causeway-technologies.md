# Causeway Technologies

*Category: Direct, United Kingdom | Researched: 2026-10-10 (re-checked)*

## 1. Snapshot

- Observed: Causeway Technologies was founded in 1999 by chief executive Phil Brown and is headquartered in Buckinghamshire, UK (https://www.causeway.com/news/causeway-acquires-letsbuild-aproplan)
- Observed: Five Arrows, the private equity arm of Rothschild & Co, invested in June 2021 and took "a significant minority stake"; the Group's directors "consider there to be no single controlling party" (https://www.rothschildandco.com/en/five-arrows/corporate-private-equity/portfolio/causeway/ ; https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/08692306/filing-history/MzUzNDExMzI3NmFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Observed: the trading company is Causeway Technologies Limited (No. 03921897), 75% or more owned by Causeway Technologies Holdings Limited, whose ultimate parent is Causeway Software Holdings Limited; founders Philip Brown and Peter Nagle are registered as having significant influence or control (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/persons-with-significant-control)
- Observed: Causeway Technologies Limited reported turnover of £76,425,062 for the year to 31 December 2025 (2024: £68,915,498) and an average of 430 employees (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/filing-history/MzUzNDExMDUyN2FkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Observed: Group annual recurring revenue was £81.8m at 31 December 2025 (2024: £74.1m), recurring software revenue was 94% of Group revenue, Group EBITDA was £24.3m, and the Group "now serves over 4,000 customers" (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/filing-history/MzUzNDExMDUyN2FkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Observed: in its December 2025 press release Causeway describes itself as serving "over 3,000 customers globally with over 500 employees" (https://www.causeway.com/news/causeway-acquires-letsbuild-aproplan)
- Observed: Causeway says CausewayOne e-Invoicing (formerly Causeway Tradex) has "60,000 + suppliers onboarded" and describes itself as "the UK's largest online trading network in construction" (https://www.causeway.com/supply-chain/e-invoicing)
- Observed: Causeway announced on 3 December 2025 that it had acquired LetsBuild Aproplan, a field management tool "trusted by over 900 customers", its "sixth strategic acquisition" since Five Arrows invested in 2021 (https://www.causeway.com/news/causeway-acquires-letsbuild-aproplan)
- Observed: the acquiring entity was Causeway Technologies Limited, which bought LetsBuild Belgium SA, with LetsBuild due to be "released to UK clients via CausewayOne in summer 2026" (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/filing-history/MzUzNDExMDUyN2FkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Inferred (assumption): the summer 2026 LetsBuild release to UK clients has shipped (could not confirm on 2026-10-10; the newsroom lists no item after 16 April 2026, searched https://www.causeway.com/news, the LetsBuild Aproplan page on causeway.com and a web search)

## 2. AI data moat

- Inferred (assumption): **Transaction flow across the UK construction supply chain, now being pushed upstream into decisions.**

- Observed: Causeway describes CausewayOne as "an AI-first platform, designed for construction", with "a construction-native intelligence model that understands how projects, suppliers and finance interact across the lifecycle" (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-for-causwayone-trading)
- Observed: CausewayOne unites Causeway's technologies so customers can "create golden threads of data" (https://www.causeway.com/causeway-one)
- Observed: the 2025 Group accounts say the move of Group products to CausewayOne was achieved by the end of 2025, with "Nearly 80% of UK client ARR" migrated, and that the platform "underpins the Group's significant AI roadmap release schedule for 2026" (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/filing-history/MzUzNDExMDUyN2FkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Observed: Supplier Management automates onboarding and accreditation checks, with "360 performance feedback tools and star ratings" feeding "shared performance dashboards" (https://www.causeway.com/products/supplier-management)
- Observed: Causeway's August 2026 blog claims AI "is already delivering up to 70% efficiency gains for AP teams and up to 50% efficiency gains across AR processes" (https://www.causeway.com/blog/why-ai-is-redefining-construction-finance)
- Observed: CausewayOne e-Invoicing reports "£18 bn+ total value of goods processed in 2023 from over 7.5m invoices" (https://www.causeway.com/supply-chain/e-invoicing)
- Inferred (assumption): the corpus is invoice and transaction shaped. Tens of thousands of suppliers moving real money through the e-Invoicing network gives Causeway something close to a price index for UK construction supply, which is adjacent to ProcurePro's AI Pricing Library thesis.
- Inferred (assumption): the data is strongest after award, in the payment cycle. Causeway is trying to reason forward from settled transactions to preconstruction decisions, which is the mirror image of ProcurePro reasoning forward from awarded packages.

## 3. Automated workflows

- Observed: a CausewayOne preconstruction webinar page states AI is "embedded into the pre-construction workflow, offering up to 75% efficiency gains", spanning "estimate creation, BoQ ingestion to automated trade mark up and supplier selection" (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-in-causewayone-preconstruction)
- Observed: that page is an on-demand webinar registration page (agenda: Matt Keen, Senior VP Construction, and a "Demo: AI driving value across the bid"), not product documentation (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-in-causewayone-preconstruction)
- Observed: a CausewayOne Trading webinar page states "Those capabilities are now live - and CausewayOne Trading is where you'll see them first", covering "real AP and AR workflows" (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-for-causwayone-trading)
- Observed: CausewayOne Estimating claims "time savings of up to 90% on BoQ production and 40% on takeoff, on average" (https://www.causeway.com/products/estimating)
- Inferred (assumption): "automated trade mark-up and supplier selection" is the line that matters, because it describes work ProcurePro also does, offered by a UK incumbent with an installed base in a market ProcurePro is scaling into. The 75% figure sits on a webinar page, and no customer case study for it was found (searched causeway.com case studies and campaign.causeway.com, 2026-10-10), so it is best held as a claim to verify.

## 4. Product overlap with ProcurePro

- Observed: Causeway Enquiries is a tender management product that "enables contractors to manage and send tenders efficiently while allowing subcontractors to receive and respond to tenders", and says it "drastically reduces the time involved in bid comparison" (https://www.causeway.com/products/tendering)
- Observed: CausewayOne Estimating offers "Bid comparison and collaboration" ("Evaluate bids from multiple suppliers and choose the best one for the project") and cites "1,755,000 + trade packages sent out per year" and "23,500 + estimates completed per year", "Trusted by over 400 organisations" (https://www.causeway.com/products/estimating)
- Observed: Supplier Management covers onboarding, "CAS-aligned PQQs" and supplier performance (https://www.causeway.com/products/supplier-management)
- Inferred (assumption): Causeway covers vendor management, supplier onboarding and compliance, and supply chain transactions well. It covers tendering, trade package distribution and bid comparison, and overlaps ProcurePro's AI Estimating Handover through estimate-to-package flow.
- Inferred (assumption): Causeway does not offer a Procurement Schedule, a Scope of Works Library, or priced trade comparison with exclusion extraction at ProcurePro's depth (searched the Enquiries, Estimating and Supplier Management product pages for exclusions, scope library and procurement schedule, 2026-10-10; none found).
- Inferred (assumption): overlap is substantial at the tender and bid-comparison stage already, and the direction of travel is straight at ProcurePro. Causeway is moving from finance upstream into AI preconstruction while ProcurePro moves from procurement outward.

- Observed: Causeway lists international offices in Paris, Lisbon, Belgium, Delaware (US), Sydney (Brookvale, New South Wales) and Bangalore (https://www.causeway.com/locations)
- Observed: Causeway Technologies Limited's 2025 accounts list a subsidiary, Causeway Technologies PTY Limited (Australia, Level 3, 261 George Street, Sydney), added in 2025, with the principal activity "Provision of software and consultancy services for construction companies" (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/03921897/filing-history/MzUzNDExMDUyN2FkaXF6a2N4/document?format=xhtml&download=1)
- Inferred (assumption): in the UK Causeway is entrenched, with a quarter-century of relationships and e-invoicing rails it says carry 60,000+ suppliers.
- Inferred (assumption): Causeway has an Australian entity and Sydney office but no evidence was found that it sells procurement or tendering there (searched causeway.com product pages and web for CausewayOne Australia tendering, 2026-10-10), so it is not yet a visible factor in ANZ, but ProcurePro should not treat it as UK only.
- Inferred (assumption): this is the strongest local incumbent in ProcurePro's most developed expansion market. Because Causeway presents as finance and supply chain software, its move upstream into supplier selection is easy to miss when scanning the procurement category.

## 7. Differentiators vs ProcurePro

- Inferred (assumption): the supplier network already exists in the UK (60,000+ suppliers onboarded to e-Invoicing, per Causeway), though how many are subcontractor trade packages rather than materials suppliers is unknown.
- Inferred (assumption): procure-to-pay in one place, which finance directors like, with e-Invoicing, Supplier Management, Estimating and Enquiries on one platform.
- Inferred (assumption): a quarter-century of UK contractor relationships and process familiarity (founded 1999, 4,000+ customers per its accounts).

## 8. Risks for ProcurePro

1. Inferred (assumption): **UK deals get reframed as procure-to-pay.** High likelihood. Causeway can argue the whole cycle where ProcurePro argues one stage of it, and the buyer is often the same commercial director.
2. Inferred (assumption): **Pricing intelligence from real invoices** is arguably a better source than awarded packages for market rates, which undercuts ProcurePro's AI Pricing Library pitch in the UK.
3. Inferred (assumption): **Roll-up acquisitions** could add depth faster than building it. The record is six acquisitions since 2021, and Causeway already runs tender and estimating products.

## 9. Opportunities for ProcurePro

- Inferred (assumption): Causeway's centre of gravity is finance and settled transactions (UK ARR, e-Invoicing) and its AI preconstruction claims are recent, so a procurement manager may not yet think of it as the tool for bid levelling with exclusions.
- Inferred (assumption): the 75% preconstruction efficiency claim has no public case study behind it (searched, 2026-10-10). A documented side-by-side on real bid levelling would favour the vendor with shipped, specific capability.
- Inferred (assumption): Causeway's revenue is about 84% UK. ProcurePro can carry one story across ANZ, UK, Middle East and North America, which suits multinational contractors like Bouygues and Multiplex.

# Causeway Technologies

*Category: Direct, United Kingdom | Researched: 2026-08-08*

## 1. Snapshot

- Observed: Causeway is founder owned, headquartered in Buckinghamshire UK, with 300+ staff worldwide and over 20 years in construction software (https://www.causeway.com/)
- Observed: the Tradex supply chain platform spans a network of 59,000 suppliers and 750 customers (https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1011016/tradex)
- Observed: Causeway acquired LetsBuild Aproplan in December 2025 to improve information flow across the CausewayOne platform (https://www.causeway.com/causeway-one)
- Observed: backed by Five Arrows, the Rothschild & Co private equity arm (https://www.rothschildandco.com/en/five-arrows/corporate-private-equity/portfolio/causeway/)

## 2. AI data moat

**Transaction flow across the UK construction supply chain, now being pushed upstream into decisions.**

- Observed: CausewayOne is positioned as an AI-first platform with a "construction-native intelligence model that understands how projects, suppliers and finance interact across the lifecycle" (https://www.causeway.com/causeway-one)
- Observed: the platform unites Causeway's products to share data and create "golden threads of data" across the project lifecycle (https://www.causeway.com/causeway-one)
- Observed: supplier management automates onboarding, compliance and performance, with 360 performance feedback, star ratings and shared performance dashboards (https://www.causeway.com/products/supplier-management)
- Inferred (assumption): the corpus is invoice and transaction shaped. 59,000 suppliers moving real money through Tradex gives Causeway something close to a price index for UK construction supply, which is uncomfortably adjacent to ProcurePro's AI Pricing Library thesis.
- Inferred (assumption): the data is strongest after award, in the payment cycle. Causeway is trying to reason forward from settled transactions to preconstruction decisions, which is the mirror image of ProcurePro reasoning forward from awarded packages.

## 3. Automated workflows

- Observed: AI is embedded in the preconstruction workflow with claimed efficiency gains of up to 75%, spanning estimate creation, BoQ ingestion, automated trade mark-up and supplier selection (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-in-causewayone-preconstruction)
- Observed: CausewayOne Trading AI capabilities are live, focused on AP and AR workflows (https://campaign.causeway.com/ai-for-causwayone-trading)
- Observed: AI recognises an invoice "down to the detail beyond human logic", and improves visibility, flags risks early and streamlines workflows in construction finance (https://www.causeway.com/blog/why-ai-is-redefining-construction-finance)
- Inferred (assumption): "automated trade mark-up and supplier selection" is the line that matters, because it describes work ProcurePro also does, offered by a UK incumbent with an installed base in a market ProcurePro is scaling into. The 75% figure appears on a campaign page rather than in product documentation, and no supporting customer case study was found, so it is best held as a claim to verify.

## 4. Product overlap with ProcurePro

Covers well: Vendor Management, supplier onboarding and compliance, supply chain transactions.
Covers partially: Tenders, supplier selection, and now estimate-to-package handover, which overlaps ProcurePro's AI Estimating Handover.
Does not cover: Procurement Schedule at ProcurePro's depth, Scope of Works Library, priced trade comparison with exclusion extraction.

- Inferred (assumption): overlap is currently partial but the direction of travel is straight at ProcurePro. Causeway is moving from finance upstream into preconstruction while ProcurePro moves from procurement outward.

## 5. Market position and geography

- **UK:** entrenched. Described as the strongest dedicated supply chain procurement option in the UK market as of 2026, with two decades of relationships and the e-invoicing rails much of the industry already runs on.
- **North America and ANZ:** limited. Not a factor in ProcurePro's other markets.
- Inferred (assumption): this is the strongest local incumbent in ProcurePro's most developed expansion market. Because Causeway presents as finance and supply chain software, its move upstream into supplier selection is easy to miss when scanning the procurement category.

## 6. Pricing & packaging

- Inferred (assumption): enterprise, quote only, priced by transaction volume or supplier count on the network side. No public pricing found.
- Inferred (assumption): PE ownership under Five Arrows plus an acquisition in December 2025 suggests a roll-up strategy, which usually means bundle-led pricing and pressure to consolidate a customer's spend.

## 7. Differentiators vs ProcurePro

- The supplier network already exists in the UK, with 59,000 suppliers transacting.
- Procure-to-pay in one place, which finance directors like.
- Twenty years of UK contractor relationships and process familiarity.

## 8. Risks for ProcurePro

1. **UK deals get reframed as procure-to-pay.** High likelihood. Causeway can argue the whole cycle where ProcurePro argues one stage of it, and the buyer is often the same commercial director.
2. **Pricing intelligence from real invoices** is arguably a better source than awarded packages for market rates, which undercuts ProcurePro's AI Pricing Library pitch in the UK.
3. **Roll-up acquisitions** could add the preconstruction depth Causeway currently lacks, faster than building it.

## 9. Opportunities for ProcurePro

- Causeway's centre of gravity is finance and settled transactions. Its preconstruction presence is recent, and it is not yet the tool a procurement manager opens to run a tender.
- The preconstruction efficiency claim has no public case study behind it. A documented side-by-side on real bid levelling would favour the vendor with shipped, specific capability.
- Causeway is UK only. ProcurePro can carry one story across ANZ, UK, Middle East and North America, which suits multinational contractors like Bouygues and Multiplex.

## 10. Open questions

- What does "automated trade mark-up and supplier selection" actually do in product, and is it GA or campaign copy?
- Is the 75% preconstruction efficiency claim backed by a customer case study?
- Does Causeway intend to build or buy priced-scope comparison?
- How much of the 59,000-supplier network is subcontractor trade packages versus materials suppliers?

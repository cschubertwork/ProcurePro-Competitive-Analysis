# Procore

*Category: Direct (bundler) | Researched: 2026-08-08*

## 1. Snapshot

- Observed: Procore is used by 16,000+ companies globally and bundles procurement workflows, subcontractor bidding and purchase order tracking inside a broader project management platform (https://www.procuredesk.com/procurement-software-for-construction/)
- Observed: R&D spending is reported around US$300m (https://www.constructionindustry.ai/product/procore-ai-copilot-agents-review/)
- Observed: ProcurePro integrates with Procore and markets the pairing publicly, so the relationship is partner and competitor at once (https://procurepro.co/blog/procore-and-procurepro-are-better-connected)

## 2. AI data moat

**Breadth of project system-of-record data, plus bought capability to act on it.**

- Observed: Procore acquired Datagrid, a vertical AI firm, announced 20 January 2026. Datagrid's agents cover deep search, RFI validation, scope checks, prequalification, audits and daily reports. Founder Thiago da Costa now leads Procore's AI and data strategy (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/)
- Observed: Procore's stated aim is to let customers "bridge the gaps between siloed data" and connect third-party sources such as ERP and cloud storage, which "unlocks the true value of construction data" (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/)
- Inferred (assumption): Procore's moat is horizontal reach rather than depth. It sees the whole project but not the commercial detail of how a trade package was priced and awarded, because that decision usually happens before or outside Procore.
- Inferred (assumption): the Datagrid purchase is a distribution move as much as a technology one. Products will be sold to non-Procore customers too, which extends the agent layer beyond the installed base.

## 3. Automated workflows

**The most aggressive agentic positioning in the category.**

- Observed: Procore launched Procore AI with embedded agents automating RFIs, submittals, contract reviews and project workflows, with human oversight retained (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-launches-procore-ai-with-new-agents-to-boost-construction-management-efficiency)
- Observed: on 23 July 2026 Procore announced Digital Coworker packages, an expanded AI Agent Library, and previewed Procore Skills, which lets organisations teach Procore AI their own processes, standards and best practices (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Inferred (assumption): Procore Skills is the strategically dangerous one. If a customer can encode their own procurement process into Procore, the argument for a specialist procurement tool weakens for firms that only need "good enough".
- Inferred (assumption): shipped agent coverage is still concentrated on RFIs, submittals and contract review. Procurement and bid levelling are not where the announced agents land.

## 4. Product overlap with ProcurePro

Covers well: nothing at ProcurePro's depth.
Covers partially: Tenders, Contracts, Vendor Management, purchase orders, all as modules inside a wider platform.
Does not cover: Scope of Works Library, priced trade comparison, procurement-specific scheduling and analytics.

- Inferred (assumption): the overlap is commercial rather than functional. Procore rarely wins on procurement capability, it wins when a customer decides one platform is worth more than the best tool.

## 5. Market position and geography

- **North America:** entrenched incumbent.
- **UK and ANZ:** strong presence, widely deployed with tier one and mid-tier contractors.
- **Middle East:** present and growing.
- Inferred (assumption): Procore is in every market ProcurePro is entering and will be in the room on every deal, usually as the platform the buyer already owns.

## 6. Pricing & packaging

- Inferred (assumption): annual subscription priced on construction volume, quote only, with modules bundled. Procurement is rarely a separately justified line item, which means the marginal cost of "just use Procore's module" looks like zero to a buyer.
- Observed: Digital Coworker packages introduce a new packaging layer for AI adoption (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)

## 7. Differentiators vs ProcurePro

- One platform, one contract, one vendor relationship.
- Broadest agent library shipping today.
- Already installed at most of ProcurePro's target buyers, so no procurement process to run.

## 8. Risks for ProcurePro

1. **The bundling squeeze.** Highest likelihood and the standing risk. Procore does not have to be better, only close enough to make a second contract hard to justify.
2. **Procore Skills lets customers self-serve the workflow depth** that ProcurePro currently sells.
3. **Partner-to-competitor drift.** The integration that helps ProcurePro today also gives Procore a clear view of where the procurement gap sits in its own platform.

## 9. Opportunities for ProcurePro

- Procore's agents are pointed at RFIs, submittals and contract review, not at the award decision. That gap is currently unattended by the largest player.
- The integration is real leverage while it lasts. "Keep Procore, add the procurement depth it does not have" is an easier sale than a rip and replace.
- Procore's breadth means procurement will always be a module competing internally for roadmap. ProcurePro's whole roadmap is procurement.

## 10. Open questions

- Is a procurement or bid-levelling agent on the announced Procore roadmap? Nothing found in public announcements to date.
- What happens to the ProcurePro integration if Procore ships competing depth?
- How much of Datagrid's agent capability is available to non-Procore customers, and at what price?

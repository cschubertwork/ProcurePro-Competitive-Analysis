# Procore

*Category: Direct (bundler) | Researched: 2026-10-10 (re-checked)*

## 1. Snapshot

- Observed: Procore is listed on the NYSE (PCOR). Its Q2 2026 revenue was $375 million, up 16% year over year (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026050615/pcor-q226x8xkxexx991.htm)
- Observed: Procore's full-year 2025 revenue was $1,322.5 million, of which 85% was billed to U.S. customers (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026011055/pcor-20251231.htm)
- Observed: Procore reports 2,871 organic customers contributing more than $100,000 of annual recurring revenue as of 30 June 2026, up 14% year over year. It does not give a total customer count in the filings checked (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026050615/pcor-q226x8xkxexx991.htm)
- Observed: Procore's research and development expense was $362.4 million in FY2025, up from $313.0 million in FY2024 and $300.6 million in FY2023 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026011055/pcor-20251231.htm)
- Observed: Procore's Preconstruction products include Bid Management and Prequalification, and its bid management page says a chosen bid can be converted "into a subcontract or purchase order" (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026011055/pcor-20251231.htm, https://www.procore.com/bid-management)
- Observed: ProcurePro has a listing in the Procore App Marketplace, built and supported by ProcurePro, showing 209 installs on 2026-10-10 (https://marketplace.procore.com/apps/procurepro)
- Inferred (assumption): the relationship is partner and competitor at once. ProcurePro markets the integration publicly, while Procore ships its own bid management and, since 2026, bid and procurement agents (see sections 3 and 4).

## 2. AI data moat

- Inferred (assumption): **Breadth of project system-of-record data, plus bought capability to act on it.**

- Observed: Procore announced the acquisition of Datagrid, a vertical AI firm, on 20 January 2026; terms were not disclosed at announcement (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/)
- Observed: Procore's 10-Q says it completed the acquisition of all outstanding equity of Toric Labs, Inc. (d/b/a Datagrid) on 16 January 2026 for $168.0 million in cash (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026051460/pcor-20260630.htm)
- Observed: Datagrid's agents cover deep search, RFI validation, scope checks, pre-qualification, audits and daily reports, according to Construction Dive citing Datagrid's website (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/)
- Observed: Thiago da Costa, Datagrid's founder, was to lead AI and data strategy at Procore. By 23 July 2026 he is titled SVP of AI & Data (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/, https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Observed: Procore's stated aim is to let customers "bridge the gaps between siloed data" and initiate actions across their ecosystem, which "unlocks the true value of construction data" (https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-datagrid-vertical-ai-firm/810120/)
- Observed: Procore's support manual says "Datagrid is available for purchase by all organizations", so the Datagrid layer is sold beyond Procore customers (https://v2.support.procore.com/product-manuals/procore-ai/tutorials/manage-ai-skills/)
- Inferred (assumption): Procore's moat is horizontal reach rather than award-decision depth. It sees the whole project but probably not the commercial detail of how a trade package was priced and awarded when that happens outside Procore; this premise is untested.
- Inferred (assumption): the Datagrid purchase is a distribution move as much as a technology one, because it extends the agent layer beyond the installed base (premise confirmed by the "all organizations" line above).

## 3. Automated workflows

- Inferred (assumption): **The most aggressive agentic positioning in the category, and it now reaches bid levelling.**

- Observed: at Groundbreak on 20 November 2024 Procore announced Procore AI with Agents and Agent Studio, with Agents described as "available next year" (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-launches-procore-ai-with-new-agents-to-boost-construction-management-efficiency)
- Observed: on 15 October 2025 Procore described Procore Helix as its intelligence layer and said Procore Agent Builder was "Now in open beta and available to all customers" (https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/procore-advances-future-construction-new-ai-innovations-groundbreak-2025-2025-10-15)
- Observed: on 23 July 2026 Procore announced three Digital Coworker packages, now "generally available": Starter Pack with five agents, Pro with 20, and Enterprise adding Agent Studio for building custom agents (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Observed: the same release says Procore Skills, which lets organisations teach Procore AI their own processes, standards and best practices, "will begin rolling out across all Digital Coworker packages in August" (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Observed: Procore's support manual describes Skills as live and says it is "available with the purchase of a Digital Coworker package". We found no dated general-availability announcement for Skills as of 2026-10-10 (https://v2.support.procore.com/product-manuals/procore-ai/tutorials/manage-ai-skills/)
- Observed: Procore's support FAQ lists in its Pro package (20 agents) a Bid Leveling Agent, Bid Analyzer Agent, Scope Writer Agent and Procurement Log Creator Agent. The Bid Leveling Agent "automates the comparison of subcontractor bids" by "identifying missing scopes and price discrepancies" (https://v2.support.procore.com/faq-which-agents-are-included-in-my-procore-ai-plan/)
- Observed: Procore's Bid Analyzer Agent "analyzes bid submissions against project scopes, drawings, and specifications to identify gaps, exclusions, inconsistencies, and risks before award" (https://v2.support.procore.com/faq-which-agents-are-included-in-my-procore-ai-plan/)
- Observed: Procore says every action its AI proposes "is staged for a person to review and approve before anything changes in Procore" (https://v2.support.procore.com/product-manuals/procore-ai/tutorials/about-procore-ai/)
- Inferred (assumption): Procore Skills is the strategically dangerous one. If a customer can encode its own procurement process into Procore, the argument for a specialist procurement tool weakens for firms that only need "good enough".
- Inferred (assumption): the earlier view that procurement and bid levelling are "not where the announced agents land" is wrong as of 2026-10-10. Procore ships bid levelling, bid analysis and procurement-log agents; what is unconfirmed is how deep they go on priced scope libraries and award recommendation (searched Procore agent pages, support FAQ and the web for "scope of works library" and "award recommendation", 2026-10-10).

## 4. Product overlap with ProcurePro

- Observed: Procore's bid management page says users can "create, manage, level, and track bid invitations" and "Compare bids side by side to identify gaps and outliers" (https://www.procore.com/bid-management)
- Observed: Procore's agent library describes the Bid Leveling Agent as comparing "subcontractor bids side-by-side in one matrix, flagging missing scope and price gaps" (https://www.procore.com/ai/agents)
- Observed: Procore's Procurement Log Creator Agent "extracts long-lead items, material requirements, submittal dependencies, and delivery milestones from project documents to create a structured procurement log" (https://v2.support.procore.com/faq-which-agents-are-included-in-my-procore-ai-plan/)
- Observed: ProcurePro's own Procore integration page lists: sync Procore documents into tender packages, notifications when documents are added or revised, notifying subcontractors with addendums, and automatic document transmittals (https://procurepro.co/integrations/procore)
- Observed: ProcurePro's blog says users can "Access ProcurePro from within Procore" (https://procurepro.co/blog/procore-and-procurepro-are-better-connected)
- Inferred (assumption): overlap is real on bid comparison and procurement logging and is now partly agentic. Procore has no equivalent of ProcurePro's Scope of Works Library or procurement-specific analytics (searched procore.com bid management and AI agent pages, the support FAQ and a web search for "Procore scope of works library", 2026-10-10, nothing found).
- Inferred (assumption): the overlap is commercial as much as functional. Procore wins when a customer decides one platform is worth more than the best tool, and bundled packages make that easier (Procore's FY2025 10-K says customers "will choose one or more of the bundled packages").

## 5. Market position and geography

- Observed: Procore says "over three million projects have run on Procore across 150+ countries" (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Observed: 85% of FY2025 revenue and 85% of H1 2026 revenue was billed in the U.S.; the rest of the world was 15% (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026051460/pcor-20260630.htm)
- Observed: Procore lists international sales and marketing offices in Sydney, Australia; Toronto, Canada; London, England; Dublin, Ireland; and Dubai, UAE, and calls international expansion "a major, and largely greenfield, opportunity" (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026051460/pcor-20260630.htm)
- Observed: Procore runs a dedicated Australia and New Zealand site and a United Kingdom site (https://www.procore.com/en-au)
- Inferred (assumption): Procore is in every market ProcurePro is entering and will be in the room on many deals, usually as the platform the buyer already owns. Offices and the 15% non-U.S. revenue share show presence but not how widely it is deployed with tier one UK or ANZ contractors (could not confirm on 2026-10-10).

## 6. Pricing & packaging

- Observed: Procore's pricing page offers "a custom quote" and says every annual contract has "fixed, predictable pricing" with unlimited users (https://www.procore.com/pricing)
- Observed: Procore's FY2025 10-K says customers "will choose one or more of the bundled packages and a tier within each package" instead of picking individual products (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000162828026011055/pcor-20251231.htm)
- Observed: Digital Coworker packages (Starter Pack, Pro, Enterprise) are a separate packaging layer for AI, and each includes Control Tower for visibility into usage and credit consumption (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Observed: Procore's pricing page says "We charge an upfront annual fee by product and based upon your Annual Construction Volume (ACV) - the aggregate dollar value of the construction work across your projects." (https://www.procore.com/pricing)
- Inferred (assumption): procurement is rarely a separately justified line item, so the marginal cost of "just use Procore's module" looks near zero to a buyer.

## 7. Differentiators vs ProcurePro

- Inferred (assumption): one platform, one contract, one vendor relationship.
- Inferred (assumption): the broadest agent library in the category (20 agents in Pro, per Procore's support FAQ; other vendors' libraries were not counted).
- Inferred (assumption): already installed at many of ProcurePro's target buyers, so no procurement process to run (not quantified; could not confirm on 2026-10-10).

## 8. Risks for ProcurePro

1. Inferred (assumption): **the bundling squeeze.** Highest likelihood and the standing risk. Procore does not have to be better, only close enough to make a second contract hard to justify.
2. Inferred (assumption): **Procore's bid and procurement agents plus Skills let customers self-serve workflow depth** that ProcurePro currently sells.
3. Inferred (assumption): **partner-to-competitor drift.** The integration that helps ProcurePro today also gives Procore a clear view of the procurement gap in its own platform. Procore also now owns DroneDeploy (see section 10) and is spending on acquisitions.

## 9. Opportunities for ProcurePro

- Inferred (assumption): Procore's agents compare and analyse bids, but a priced scope library and an award recommendation built on a large base of awarded packages are not visible in Procore's published agents (searched 2026-10-10, see section 4). The gap is narrower than previously stated.
- Inferred (assumption): the integration is real leverage while it lasts. "Keep Procore, add the procurement depth it does not have" is an easier sale than a rip and replace.
- Inferred (assumption): Procore's breadth means procurement competes internally for roadmap. ProcurePro's whole roadmap is procurement.

## 10. Open questions

- Observed: Procore completed the acquisition of DroneDeploy on 9 September 2026 for approximately $845.0 million in cash (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611052/000119312526386428/d59216d8k.htm)
- Observed: Procore's Groundbreak 2026 is scheduled for Orlando, 21 to 22 October 2026 (https://www.procore.com/groundbreak)
- Inferred (assumption): open question, how deep is the Bid Leveling Agent on priced scope, exclusions and award recommendation, and does it work on non-Procore quote documents? Public pages do not say.
- Inferred (assumption): open question, what happens to the ProcurePro integration if Procore ships competing depth at Groundbreak 2026?
- Inferred (assumption): open question, at what price do non-Procore customers buy Datagrid? The support manual says it is available to all organizations but gives no price.

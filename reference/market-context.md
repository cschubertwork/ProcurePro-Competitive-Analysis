# Market context: facts the analysis relies on that belong to no profile

*Researched: 2026-10-10. Each item names where the analysis or README uses it, then sources it with a verbatim quote or labels it Inferred.*

## Spend committed early

- Observed: ProcurePro's homepage says "Take control of procurement. 90% of project spend is committed early, locking in risk and profit." (https://procurepro.co/)
- Observed: ProcurePro's May 2026 funding release says "By the time procurement closes, 80% of a project's cost is already committed and the supply chain risk profile is locked in." (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026)
- Inferred (assumption): the two figures conflict (90% "committed early" versus 80% "by the time procurement closes"); they may measure different things, but ProcurePro uses both. The repo's product-info.md and any public use should pick one and attribute it as "ProcurePro states". Neither is an independent statistic, and no third-party source for either was found (searched ProcurePro pages and web search, 2026-10-10).
- Used in: `reference/product-info.md` (problem 5) and the framing that ProcurePro "sits inside the moment project value is committed".

## Market size and margins

- Observed: ProcurePro states "Construction is an $18 trillion (US$13 trillion) global industry, and one of the least profitable, operating on slim margins of just 1 to 4 per cent." (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026)
- Inferred (assumption): this is a vendor claim with no cited source on the page; the A$ figure is a currency conversion of the US$13 trillion stated. Not used in the analysis text; recorded because the dashboard may carry it.

## Scale claims that frame the moat comparison

- Observed: ProcurePro's homepage says "Trusted by leading contractors on $100+ billion in projects" (https://procurepro.co/)
- Inferred (assumption): the analysis's "A$130bn, 6,000 projects, 200,000 packages" comes from product-info.md and a different page; the homepage figure ($100+ billion) is lower than A$130bn. Currency (A$ versus US$) may explain some of the gap; the lead should confirm which figure ProcurePro currently states.
- Observed: E1 states "In 2025, Aussie Head Contractors posted 24,000 tenders at a total value of $354 billion!" (https://estimateone.com/about-us/); this is the nearest published comparable for the A$130bn claim and sits in the same market
- Observed: Consight states "$12B+ in project value managed on Consight" (https://home.consight.ai/)

## The shift "from reading documents to executing workflow steps in about twelve months"

- Inferred (assumption): this is the analysis's own judgement and the README's headline conclusion. It is unsourced as stated.
- Observed: Procore launched Procore AI with agents on 20 November 2024 (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-launches-procore-ai-with-new-agents-to-boost-construction-management-efficiency)
- Observed: Procore announced "Digital Coworker packages and 20 new pre-built AI agents" on 23 July 2026 (https://www.procore.com/press/procore-introduces-digital-coworker-packages-expands-ai-agent-library-and-previews-skills-to-help-construction-teams-put-ai-to-work)
- Inferred (assumption): the primary dates put Procore's first agents about 20 months before the analysis (August 2026), and the expansion in July 2026. "About twelve months" is therefore loose; "since late 2024" is what the sources support for Procore, and the lead should reword or label the figure.
- Observed: E1 documents AI quote analysis dated 13 April 2026 (https://builder.support.estimateone.com/en/articles/1756366327-quote-analysis), so reading-and-comparing is now shipping in ProcurePro's home market too, which weakens the framing that ProcurePro leads on document AI locally

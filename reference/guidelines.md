# Profile Guidelines: ProcurePro Competitive Research

Every competitor profile uses the sections below, in this order. The template is tuned to this run's framing: **AI data moat** and **automated workflows**, read against the markets ProcurePro is expanding into.

## Labelling rules

- `- Observed: [claim] ([URL])` for anything sourced from public information
- `- Inferred (assumption): [claim]` for interpretation, judgement or an educated guess
- Never state an Observed claim without a URL. If there is no URL, it is Inferred.
- No em dashes. Use hyphens.

## Sections

### 1. Snapshot

- Company, HQ, founding year, ownership or listing status
- Scale markers: headcount, customers, revenue or ARR, funding
- One line on what they actually sell

### 2. AI data moat

The central question for this run. Capture:

- What proprietary corpus do they sit on, and how did they get it?
- Is the data supply-side (vendors, bids, financials), demand-side (projects, spend) or transactional (invoices, orders)?
- Does the corpus compound with usage, or is it a static archive?
- How defensible is it? Could a well-funded competitor approximate it from public or purchased data?
- What have they actually shipped that uses it, as opposed to announced?

### 3. Automated workflows

- Which procurement steps are automated today, and to what depth: extraction, comparison, recommendation, or execution?
- Agentic framing: do they claim agents that take actions, or assistants that produce output for a human?
- Named products, launch dates, and whether they are GA, beta or preview
- Efficiency claims, with the number and the source

### 4. Product overlap with ProcurePro

- Which of ProcurePro's eight modules this competitor covers, and how well
- Where the overlap is real versus superficial
- What they do that ProcurePro does not

### 5. Market position and geography

- Which of ProcurePro's target markets they occupy: ANZ, UK, North America, Middle East
- Strength of position in each, and direction of travel
- Distribution advantages: channel, partnerships, installed base

### 6. Pricing & packaging

- Model, published prices if any, and what drives cost up
- Whether pricing is transparent or quote-only
- Any free tier and what it is doing strategically

### 7. Differentiators vs ProcurePro

- The honest case for choosing them over ProcurePro

### 8. Risks for ProcurePro

- How this competitor could damage ProcurePro's plan, ranked by likelihood

### 9. Opportunities for ProcurePro

- What their weaknesses or neglect opens up

### 10. Open questions

- What could not be resolved from public sources, and what would settle it

## Category definitions

- **Direct** - competes for the same budget and the same job to be done
- **Direct (bundler)** - wins by including a procurement module inside a wider platform, not by being better at procurement
- **Adjacent** - overlapping thesis and technology, different buyer or different part of the supply chain

These are definitions used in this repo, not facts about the companies.

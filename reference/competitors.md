# Competitors: ProcurePro

Scope for this run: **top 5 only**, with at least one Australian competitor and several global players chosen to match where ProcurePro is expanding (UK, North America, Middle East).

| Name | Category | Website | Home market | Primary segments | Why on the list |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Autodesk (BuildingConnected + TradeTapp) | Direct | construction.autodesk.com | US, global | GCs and subcontractors, commercial construction | Closest functional overlap on bid management and subcontractor qualification, sitting on the largest supply-side network. The main obstacle to a US entry. |
| Procore | Direct (bundler) | procore.com | US, global | GCs, owners, specialty contractors | Platform incumbent in every market ProcurePro is entering, with a procurement module and an aggressive agentic AI push. Also an integration partner. |
| Felix Group (ASX:FLX) | Direct | felix.net | Australia | Construction, mining, utilities, infrastructure, government, property | The Australian rival, headquartered in the same city as ProcurePro. Vendor management plus sourcing, with Nexvia serving contractors. |
| Causeway Technologies | Direct (UK) | causeway.com | United Kingdom | UK contractors, housebuilders, suppliers | Entrenched on UK construction supply chain and finance rails, now moving upstream into preconstruction and supplier selection. Collides with ProcurePro's UK growth. |
| Kojo | Adjacent | usekojo.com | US | Specialty subcontractors buying materials | Different buyer, same thesis. The clearest example of a procurement data moat built from transaction flow, and a live signal of what US buyers now expect from procurement AI. |

## Category definitions

- **Direct** - competes for the same budget and the same job to be done
- **Direct (bundler)** - wins by including a procurement module inside a wider platform, not by being better at procurement
- **Adjacent** - overlapping thesis and technology, different buyer or different part of the supply chain

## Deliberately excluded

- **Payapps** - progress claims, downstream of procurement, and an existing ProcurePro integration partner. Now Autodesk-owned, so the competitive risk is captured under Autodesk.
- **Buildertrend, Simpro, Archdesk, Tradogram, Precoro** - residential, SMB or horizontal spend management. Wrong buyer.
- **Trimble Viewpoint, RIB, Oracle** - construction ERP. Adjacent systems of record that ProcurePro integrates with rather than replaces.

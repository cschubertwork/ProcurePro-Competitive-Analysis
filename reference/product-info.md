## One-sentence description

ProcurePro is procurement software built for commercial head contractors, covering the subcontractor buying cycle from procurement schedule through tender, comparison, award and contract.

## Target customers

- Head contractors in commercial construction, mid-size through tier one
- Procurement managers, commercial managers and construction executives
- Named customers: Bouygues, Multiplex, Wates, Roberts Co, Hansen Yuncken, Richard Crookes Constructions, AW Edwards, Hutchinson Builders

## Problems solved

1. Procurement runs on spreadsheets and email, so status is invisible until something slips
2. Scope gaps in trade packages surface late, after the money is committed
3. Comparing subcontractor quotes by hand is slow and inconsistent between estimators
4. Contract and approval trails are scattered across systems
5. Around 90% of project spend is committed early, which locks in risk and margin before anyone can see the picture

## Core product areas

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Procurement Schedule | Live status across all trade packages |
| Scope of Works Library | Reusable scopes to prevent omissions |
| Tenders & Price Breakdowns | Structured tendering and comparable pricing |
| Compare & Recommend | Side-by-side selection and recommendation |
| Contracts & eSignature | Contract generation and execution |
| Vendor Management | Subcontractor capacity, performance, insight |
| Lessons Learnt | Captured project learnings |
| Data & Analytics | Portfolio-level procurement reporting |

## AI product suite

- **BidLevel** - reads supplier quotes, builds a full price breakdown and extracts pricing plus exclusions in as little as 3 minutes, drawing on ProcurePro's proprietary procurement dataset
- **AI CoPilot** - always-on procurement assistant
- **AI Pricing Library** - live pricing intelligence from historical purchasing data
- **AI Estimating Handover** - connects estimating to delivery

## Proprietary data position

- 6,000+ construction projects
- A$130bn+ of construction value
- 200,000+ trade packages processed

This is the asset the AI roadmap is built on: real awarded commercial terms, not estimates.

## Primary differentiator

Depth in one job: the head contractor's subcontractor award decision. Competitors either bundle a shallow procurement module into a wider platform, or sit on a different part of the supply chain.

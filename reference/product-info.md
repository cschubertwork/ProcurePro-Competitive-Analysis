# Product Info: ProcurePro

*Researched: 2026-10-10 (re-checked). Compiled from public sources. Every claim is labelled Observed (with a URL and a verbatim quote held in the evidence file) or Inferred (assumption). Figures a vendor publishes about itself are vendor claims, not verified outcomes.*

## Snapshot

- Observed: ProcurePro says it was "Founded in Brisbane in 2020" (https://procurepro.co/news/seed-group-and-procurepro-collaborate-to-advance-construction-procurement-and-strengthen-uae-project-delivery). Its about-us timeline opens with "2020 Built on belief" and "2021 H1 Launched, first builders live" (https://procurepro.co/about-us). An earlier version of this file said 2019; no source supports that.
- Observed: The Australian contracting entity is Blenktech Pty Ltd, ABN 12 620 650 553, "trading as ProcurePro" (ProcurePro SaaS Supply Terms [AU], v28, 19 Aug 2026, https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6899611287ee2a05883ed7ef/6a85020f213d6754e1e24e60_SaaS%20Supply%20Terms%20%5BAU%5D%20-%20ProcurePro%20v28%20-%2019%20Aug%202026.pdf).
- Observed: The ABN register lists Blenktech Pty Ltd as an Australian Private Company, ABN "Active from 24 Jul 2017", main business location QLD 4000, with the business name "Procure Pro" registered from 04 Jan 2021 (https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/12620650553, extracted 10 Oct 2026). The 2017 date is the entity's, not the product's; the same register shows an earlier trading name, "Hyra IQ", from 05 Jan 2018.
- Observed: A separate company, PROCURE PRO HOLDINGS PTY LTD (ABN 72 650 689 375, ACN 650 689 375), is "Active from 16 Jun 2021", Australian Private Company, QLD 4000 (https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/72650689375).
- Inferred (assumption): PROCURE PRO HOLDINGS PTY LTD is the group holding company (could not confirm on 2026-10-10: the ABN register gives no role or shareholding, and a web search found no source stating it).
- Observed: The UK supplier is ProcurePro UK Limited, Company No. 15155594, "Incorporated on 22 September 2023", registered office 128 City Road, London, Active (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/15155594). ProcurePro's UK terms name it as the supplier "trading as ProcurePro" (SaaS Supply Terms [UK], v28, 19 Aug 2026).
- Observed: Headquarters are described as Brisbane; the funding release is datelined "LONDON | BRISBANE | DUBAI" and says ProcurePro is "scaling existing offices in Brisbane, London and Dubai" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026).
- Observed: Five founders appear on the executive team: Alastair Blenkin (Founder & CEO), Tim Rogers (Co-Founder & Head of Product), Tom Newby (Co-Founder, "Head of Data/AI" in the card heading and "Head of Product/AI" in the bio on the same page), Nathan Denchman (Co-Founder & Head of Engineering) and Jesse Dymond (Co-Founder & Head of Design) (https://procurepro.co/about-us).
- Observed: Board as listed on the about-us page: Alastair Blenkin, John Henderson (Airtree), Paul Perrett (Glitch Capital) and Nick Capell (QIC) (https://procurepro.co/about-us).
- Observed: A third-party alumni page says the company has "over 100 employees globally" (https://www.unioncollegeuq.com.au/news-and-events/alumni/procurepro/), and Startup Daily reports "ProcurePro plans to hire 100 people over the next 24 months" (https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/funding/procurepro-raises-11m-qic-ventures-bouygues-cap-table/, 11 May 2026).
- Inferred (assumption): headcount is roughly 100 to 115 (could not confirm on 2026-10-10; no primary source states it). The earlier "104 as at 30 June 2026" figure was removed. Dealroom lists 113, an aggregator and a weak source.
- Observed: Series B, announced 5 May 2026 on ProcurePro's site: "$15 million (US$11 million) in funding led by QIC Ventures" and "The round values the company at more than $100 million" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026). The US-dollar wire version states "more than US$80 million" (https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20260511EN54923), so the A$100m and US$80m figures are the same valuation in two currencies.

## One-sentence description

- Observed: ProcurePro describes itself as "the first AI-powered, end-to-end procurement control centre built for construction", used by "construction main contractors" to "compare, select, approve and contract with vendors on a single platform" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026).
- Inferred (assumption): in comparison terms it is procurement software for commercial head contractors covering the subcontractor buying cycle from procurement schedule through tender, comparison, award and contract.

## Target customers

- Observed: ProcurePro's own case-study index lists these customers: Purcell, Clegg, Apex Contractors, Legacie, SMD, Kori Construction, Mount Anvil, Infinity Constructions, McNab, 2Construct, Erilyan, Balmain & Co., Kapitol Group, Monarch Building Solutions, Building Engineering, Jardon Group, 5Point Projects and Contract Administrators (https://procurepro.co/case-studies).

- Observed: Bouygues is a customer as well as an investor; Bouygues Group's SVP Innovation says ProcurePro "has been deployed successfully on some Bouygues projects, with usage progressively developing across several business units" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026).
- Observed: The homepage "Trusted by leading contractors" logo strip carries image labels (alt text) for Bouygues, Multiplex, Wates, AW Edwards and Richard Crookes Construction (https://procurepro.co/). The pricing page logo list includes "Bouygues UK", "Richard Crookes" and "AW Edwards" (https://procurepro.co/pricing). A logo is a weaker signal than a case study or a named quote: it shows ProcurePro chooses to display the name, not the scale or status of the account.
- Observed: The pricing page carries a customer quote from "Jason McLauchlan, Operations Manager, Novati Constructions" (https://procurepro.co/pricing).
- Observed: Reds10 and Purcell are named UK and Irish adopters in ProcurePro news items (https://procurepro.co/news, "Reds10 has joined the movement towards digital procurement"; "Purcell leads the way: First Irish contractor to adopt digital procurement").
- Inferred (assumption): Hansen Yuncken is not a customer ProcurePro displays. It was named as a customer in the earlier version of this file, and ProcurePro's homepage, pricing page, about-us and case-study index show no Hansen Yuncken logo or mention (checked 2026-10-10), so it is dropped.
- Inferred (assumption): the buyer is the commercial or procurement lead at a mid-size to tier-one head contractor (procurement managers, commercial managers, construction executives), from the roles quoted on the solution pages.

## Problems solved

- Observed: ProcurePro's homepage frames the problem as manual procurement: "Manual, fragmented procurement locks in cost & risk early across Excel & email" and "20+ fragmented & disconnected processes" (https://procurepro.co/).
- Observed: The homepage states "90% of project spend is committed early, locking in risk and profit" (https://procurepro.co/). This is ProcurePro's own marketing figure and it is not stable across its own material: the Series B release says "80% of a project's cost is already committed" by the time procurement closes (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026).
- Observed: Scope gaps are a named problem: "Scope of works gaps cost you money" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/scope-of-works).
- Observed: Manual quote comparison is a named problem: "We lose time manhandling them into a comparable format" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/comparisons-and-recommendations).
- Observed: Late or missing contracts are a named problem: "Subbies start on-site without a contract far too often" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/contract-creation).
- Observed: Procurement status is invisible without manual chasing: "To find out status, we're physically picking up the phone, sending emails, or tapping someone on the shoulder" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/procurement-schedule).

## AI product suite

- Observed: BidLevel: "BidLevel reads supplier quotes, builds a complete breakdown, and pulls out pricing and exclusions - in as fast as 3 minutes" (https://procurepro.co/). The "3 minutes" is a ProcurePro marketing claim, not a measured result. The BidLevel page says "In minutes, not hours" and "BidLevel is highly accurate for a first-pass comparison, but like all AI, it requires human judgement" (https://procurepro.co/bidlevel).
- Observed: BidLevel is also offered stand-alone and free to try: "BidLevel is free to try for anyone... No email or credit card required" and "Non-ProcurePro users can sign up directly and run their first package" (https://procurepro.co/bidlevel). The page also says "BidLevel's price extraction runs directly inside ProcurePro's vendor selection module".
- Observed: BidLevel's own usage figures: "250,000+ scope line items extracted", "4000+ bids made comparable", "95% positive rating across 250+ companies" (https://procurepro.co/bidlevel). Vendor claims.
- Observed: BidLevel has its own supply terms, "BidLevel - Supply Terms - v3 - 27 Aug 2026", naming Blenktech Pty Ltd and ProcurePro UK Limited as the providers (https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6899611287ee2a05883ed7ef/6a8fdd280ec37c4f7d2a6021_BidLevel%20-%20Supply%20Terms%20-%20v3%20-%2027%20Aug%202026.pdf).
- Observed: The always-on assistant is called AI Pulse, not "AI CoPilot": "AI Pulse: Always-on procurement assistant" and "AI-powered search that answers questions in plain language" (https://procurepro.co/, https://procurepro.co/solutions/ai-pulse). The earlier label "AI CoPilot" does not appear on the site (checked 2026-10-10) and is corrected.
- Observed: AI Pricing Library: "Access real rates & benchmarks from your past projects" and "AI-powered search across historical quotes and trade packages" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/ai-pricing-library).
- Observed: AI Estimating Handover: "Bridge the gap between estimating and delivery" (https://procurepro.co/solutions/estimating-handover).
- Observed (new since 2026-08-08): ProcurePro has an MCP connector for AI assistants: "ProcurePro's MCP connector is read-only by design" and "people sign in with their own ProcurePro account" (https://procurepro.co/blog/mcp-servers-construction-procurement, published 28 September 2026, by James Metcalfe). Whether it is generally available or limited, and any price, is not stated.
- Observed: ProcurePro publishes an AI Policy dated 9 July 2026, linked from its site footer (https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6899611287ee2a05883ed7ef/6a50333eb5ea94a2b0ed8b55_AI%20Policy%20-%20ProcurePro%20v4%20-%209%20July%202026.pdf).

## Proprietary data position

- Observed: ProcurePro's own release says: "Over the past six years, ProcurePro has been deployed across more than 6,000 construction projects globally, worth over $130 billion in construction value. The platform has processed more than 200,000 trade packages" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026). The $130bn is Australian dollars; the US wire version of the same release reads "worth more than $90 billion" (https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20260511EN54923).
- Observed: The homepage states a smaller, rounder figure: "Trusted by leading contractors on $100+ billion in projects" (https://procurepro.co/). Both claims are vendor-reported and are not independently audited.
- Observed: ProcurePro says this dataset "now underpins ProcurePro's AI product roadmap" (https://procurepro.co/news/capital-raise-2026).
- Inferred (assumption): the data is real awarded commercial terms rather than estimates, because it comes from live tendering and contract workflow; ProcurePro does not describe the data's make-up in those words (could not confirm on 2026-10-10).

## Primary differentiator

- Observed: ProcurePro describes its niche as "Purpose-built for subcontractor procurement, at the point cost and margin are locked in" (https://procurepro.co/).
- Inferred (assumption): depth in one job, the head contractor's subcontractor award decision, is its differentiator against wider platforms that bundle a shallower procurement module. This is a comparison judgement; no source states it.
